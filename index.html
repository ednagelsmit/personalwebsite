About Me

Academic Background

I am an academic specializing in early modern art history and religious culture. Trained as an art and architectural historian (PhD University of Ghent and Leiden University, 2014), my research focuses on the historical functions of early modern buildings and objects in their cultural, political, and religious contexts and is most concerned with the capacity of art and architecture to transform the beholder.

Research

My mission as a researcher is to rethink how we approach history through fruitfully combining insights from different disciplines. I study the relations between art and architecture, religion, and politics, and aim to advance the integration of these fields of historical inquiry.

Professional Experience

Beyond my academic work, I am an active and passionate tour guide across Europe and the world. I combine my expertise in art history and cultural history to create immersive, educational experiences of bespoke travelling.

Tour Experiences

Explore the masterpieces of Florence, Rome, and Venice. Discover the stories behind iconic artworks and the cultural context that shaped the Renaissance.
