About Me

Academic Background

I am an academic specializing in early modern art history and religious culture. Trained as an art and architectural historian (PhD University of Ghent and Leiden University, 2014), my research focuses on the historical functions of early modern buildings and objects in their cultural, political, and religious contexts and is most concerned with the capacity of art and architecture to transform the beholder. Through rigorous scholarly analysis and primary source research, I explore how visual art shaped religious experience and political and social practices.

Research

My mission as a researcher is to rethink how we approach history through fruitfully combining insights from different disciplines. I study the relations between art and architecture, religion, and politics, and aim to advance the integration of these fields of historical inquiry.

Professional Experience

Beyond my academic work, I am an active and passionate tour guide across Europe. I combine my expertise in art history and cultural history to create immersive, educational experiences for travelers. Leading tours allows me to share my love of heritage and foster deeper appreciation for the regions I guide.
